Squawk Ops — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Squawk Ops (the "Game", package it.pernisoft.squawkops) handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Game on Android and iOS.

In short

  • No account and no registration. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, photos or location.
  • There is no chat and no way to talk to other players.
  • Your progress is saved on your device, with a backup copy on our game server (no name or e-mail attached) so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new phone.
  • The Game shows ads, but never during a mission. Players under 16 only get non-personalized ads (child-directed ads under 13), and nobody gets personalized ads without saying yes first.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. Players under 13 must pass a parental check before any purchase.

1. Who is responsible

The Game is published by Pernisoft, Via Altichiero 56, 37059 Zevio (VR), Italy ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact: pernisoft@gmail.com.

2. The age screen

The first time you open the Game it asks for your year of birth. The year is not stored or sent anywhere: the Game keeps only your age group (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 and over), on your device. We use this to decide which protections apply (see section 6). You can be asked again by reinstalling the Game.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves on your phone or tablet: game progress, currencies and items, settings (sound, language, controls), the age group from the age screen, your choices about personalized ads and game statistics, and the order numbers of purchases already delivered (so a purchase is never delivered twice or lost). The save file is protected against tampering. A copy of the save file is also kept on our game server (see section 4, Unity Gaming Services), linked only to a random player ID, so your progress is not lost if you reinstall the Game or change phone. Uninstalling the Game deletes the copy on your device; the server copy is deleted from the Game itself or on request (section 9).

5. Ads, game statistics and your consent

Players 16 and over are asked once whether they agree to personalized ads (ads chosen using information about their device and interests). "Yes" and "No" are equally easy to choose, and the Game works the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Personalized ads. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Ads are never shown during a mission. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Every player is also asked once whether the Game may send anonymous game statistics (section 4). Players under 16 need a grown-up to agree: answering "Yes" opens the parental check. Nothing is sent before the answer, or after a "No". You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Game statistics; turning them off stops collection and asks Unity to delete the statistics already sent from your device.

6. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because younger children may still play it, it follows the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. When the age screen says a player is under 13:

  • the ad service is told the user is a child (COPPA / child-directed flags); the advertising ID is not collected, ads are never personalized, and only ad networks certified for the Google Play Families program are used;
  • a parental check (a question an adult can answer) appears before any real-money purchase and before any link that leaves the Game;
  • review requests are not shown;
  • messages from us in the Game show only text: no links, e-mail buttons or surveys;
  • game statistics are sent only if a grown-up agrees through the parental check; they contain no information that identifies the child and are never used for ads;
  • crash and error reports (section 4) contain only technical information about the error and the device, nothing that identifies the child.

Players aged 13 to 15 are never asked about personalized ads and never get them, wherever they live: 16 is the highest age at which young people can give this consent themselves under EU law.

If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

7. Legal bases (EU / UK users)

  • Providing the Game, the backup of your progress and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you.
  • Personalized ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (section 5).
  • Game statistics: your consent (or a grown-up's, under 16), which you can withdraw at any time (section 5).
  • Crash and error reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working.
  • Non-personalized ads, fraud and cheat prevention: our legitimate interest in funding the Game and keeping it fair.

8. How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game. The server copy of your progress and the purchase checks are kept as long as you may want your progress back, and deleted on request. Information held by the other services in section 4 is kept as described in their own policies. If you contact us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to answer you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Californian law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold an account or a profile about you: the only information we keep is the backup of your progress and the purchase checks, linked to a random player ID. To delete them yourself, open the Game's Settings > Account > Save and data > Delete account and data: the player ID is deleted from our game server together with the progress backup and the scores on the Game's leaderboards, the Game asks Unity to delete the game statistics sent from your device, and the progress on your device is erased. Purchase checks then keep only a scrambled order number tied to the deleted ID, so a purchase can never be delivered twice. You can also write to us at the address in section 1 and include your agent ID, shown at the bottom of the Game's Settings (tap it to copy it); the steps are also on the page Delete your account and data. Requests about the other services in section 4 are best made to them, and we will help you with any request. EU users can also complain to their data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

We do not sell personal information.

10. International transfers

The services in section 4 may process information outside your country, including in the United States. They use safeguards recognised by law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

11. Security

Information exchanged with the services in section 4 travels over encrypted connections. Your save file, on your device and on the server, is signed to detect tampering.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how children's information is handled, or needs new consent, the Game will tell you before it applies.
