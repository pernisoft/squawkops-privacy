Squawk Ops — Delete your account and data

Squawk Ops (it.pernisoft.squawkops) by Pernisoft · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Squawk Ops has no registration: your progress is linked to a random player ID (the "agent ID"). You can delete that ID and all the data linked to it at any time, in the Game or by e-mail.

In the Game

  1. Open the Game and tap the gear in the hangar (Settings).
  2. Open the Account tab, then Save and data > Manage.
  3. Tap Delete account and data, then confirm. An internet connection is needed: if the server cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and the Game tells you.

By e-mail

If you no longer have the Game, write to pernisoft@gmail.com with the subject "Squawk Ops data deletion" and your agent ID (shown at the bottom of Settings > Account; tap it to copy it). Without the agent ID we cannot find your data, because we do not know your name or e-mail. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • The agent ID and the name or codename shown on the leaderboards.
  • The copy of your progress kept on our game server (missions, rank, currencies, items, settings).
  • Your scores and records on the Game's own leaderboards (Guard Duty).
  • The game statistics sent from your device (the Game asks Unity Analytics to delete them).
  • When you delete from the Game, the progress on your device too: the Game starts again from the beginning.

What is kept

  • Purchase checks: a scrambled order number, kept so that a purchase is never delivered twice. It no longer leads to any player once the agent ID is deleted.
  • Your purchases stay in your Google Play or App Store account: after deleting, Restore purchases in Settings brings back the ones that are permanent.
  • Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards belong to your Google account: manage or delete them in the Google Play Games app.
